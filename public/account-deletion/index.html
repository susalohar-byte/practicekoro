Account & Data Deletion Request

At PracticeKoro, you own your personal data. You have the right to permanently delete your account and all associated test records at any time.

📱 Method 1: Instant In-App Account Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the PracticeKoro Android app installed on your phone, you can permanently delete your account instantly from within the app:

  1. Open the PracticeKoro app and ensure you are logged in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation menu.
  3. Tap the Settings (Gear) icon located at the top-right corner.
  4. Scroll down to the Account & Security section.
  5. Tap Delete Account (marked in red).
  6. Review the confirmation dialog and tap Permanently Delete.
Real-Time Execution: In-app deletion takes effect immediately. Your session is terminated and your data is erased from the server without recovery options.

🌐 Method 2: Web Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email us from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm the request came from you and delete the account within 7 working days, then reply to tell you it is done.

Email a Deletion Request

Or write to support@practicekoro.online from your registered email.

📋 Data Retention and Deletion Disclosure (Google Play Standards)

In accordance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion Policy, below is the transparent breakdown of data handling upon deletion:

Data Permanently Deleted

  • User Profile: Name, email address, phone number, avatar picture, district, and category.
  • Academic History: All mock test attempts, question choices, time records, and scorecards.
  • Personal Progress: Saved question bookmarks, study notes, and rank histories.
  • Authentication Credentials: Password hashes, login tokens, Google OAuth associations.
  • Subscriptions: Active Pro Pass status and feature access privileges.

Data Retained (Legal Compliance Only)

  • Financial Invoices & Payment IDs: Razorpay transaction IDs and GST tax records are retained exclusively to comply with statutory accounting and tax regulations under applicable Indian laws.
  • No Academic or Personal Data: No personal study history or identifying profile details are retained once deletion is executed.
